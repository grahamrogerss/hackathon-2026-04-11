What is this? AlgoGuard monitors 5 behavioral metrics derived from the RIS framework to detect compulsive-use patterns in real time. Three synthetic users simulate published research archetypes.
SVG — Session Velocity Gradient. Scroll speed acceleration. High = reflexive scrolling.
DDR — Dwell-to-Dismiss Ratio. Time on content before swiping. Low = reading nothing.
RLI — Re-entry Latency Index. Minutes before reopening the app. Low = compulsive return.
NSCR — Notification-Conditioned Response. % of sessions triggered by a push. High = conditioned behavior.
ACS — Affective Congruence Score. Feed drift toward emotional extremes. High = algorithmic manipulation.
